Secure E-Voting for India's Future

Vote India Secure is the trusted, SEBI-compliant electronic voting platform for Indian companies, shareholders, and organizations. End-to-end encrypted, transparent, and tamper-proof.

Key Features

  • SEBI Compliant: Fully compliant with SEBI (LODR) Regulations for e-voting at AGMs, EGMs, and Postal Ballots.
  • End-to-End Encryption: Military-grade encryption ensures every vote is secure and tamper-proof.
  • Shareholder Authentication: Verify shareholders via PAN, DPID/Client ID, and Folio Number.
  • Real-time Results: Live vote counting with transparent, auditable results.
  • AI-Powered Analytics: Advanced analytics dashboard for companies and scrutinizers.
  • Multi-language Support: Available in English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, and more.
  • QR Code Verification: Unique QR codes for vote verification and audit trails.
  • PDF Reports: Automated scrutinizer report generation in compliance with Companies Act 2013.

How It Works

  1. Company registers and creates a voting event (AGM/EGM/Postal Ballot).
  2. Shareholders are authenticated securely using PAN and DPID.
  3. Shareholders cast their votes through the secure platform.
  4. Votes are encrypted and counted in real-time.
  5. Scrutinizer verifies and publishes the results.
